It is not easy living with vast wealth.

People who work hard to build wealth are often unprepared for the work of managing it. Three things usually get in the way.

The windfall effect

A sale, an inheritance or a sudden rise in value arrives without warning. Managing what follows is harder than earning it.

Compliance

Tax and audit rules change often. Keeping up takes time and skill that most owners would rather spend elsewhere.

Complexity

Large wealth brings family emotion with it, and that gets in the way of clear, objective decisions.

Families with fortunes also need looking after.

You should be free to pursue your ambitions, enjoy your life and spend time with the people who matter.

A plan for every family member

Solutions shaped around each person's needs, not one template for all.

Structure that protects

Clear ownership of businesses, property and investments so nothing is left to chance.
